# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate in reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy, especially around fitness and health-related information.
- Welcome questions and make technical decisions transparent.
- Focus criticism on the work, never the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are
not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently exclude a
participant when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as
confidentially and fairly as possible.
